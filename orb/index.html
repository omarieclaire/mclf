Orb

A ritual-game · Parc Frédéric-Back, Montréal · 2025

Orb is a ritual-game I designed and facilitated outdoors at Parc Frédéric-Back for the first edition of Montreal Games Week.

It gathers a group of strangers around a shared object and a few simple rules, and gives them permission to be a little strange together — the small social opening that appears when people do something in the same place at the same time. Like most of my work, it's cooperative, played on foot and free, and made for the moment a crowd realizes it is a crowd.
