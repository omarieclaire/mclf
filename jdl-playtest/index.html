Jeu(x) de lumières testers needed

I'm making Jeu(x) de lumières, an outdoor playful thing/installation/game, as part of MUTEK's Village Numérique.

I'm working with people from the SAT, unhoused and housed local community members, people passionate about spotlights, people delighted by sound, and a bunch of curious passersby.

But we go live Thursday and we need to make sure this thing works! We could use your help. Your job would just be playing with it and letting me know if it breaks. It takes about 15-20 minutes max, and up to 4 people can play at a time.

It's a lights game, so it happens after dark, at Peace Park (Boul. Saint-Laurent, Montréal, QC H2S 3C8).

We could also use people just hanging around the space and standing in/playing as needed. I might ask "could you stand under a light for 5 minutes while I fix a bug in my code?"

Join the Signal group for that, or join just to stay updated!

Thanks so much!
